China's mobile app market continued to evolve in June 2026. While games remained the biggest revenue drivers, non-gaming apps further strengthened their monetization capabilities through subscriptions, content ecosystems, and digital services.

According to revenue estimates compiled by **[Appark](https://appark.ai/)** using public data from the App Store and Google Play, Tencent continued to dominate the rankings. Meanwhile, ByteDance achieved remarkable growth with **Douyin Select** and **CapCut**, highlighting the increasing commercial value of content and creator-focused products.

Most notably, **Douyin Select** generated an estimated **$130.4 million** in June, narrowing the gap with **Honor of Kings**, the highest-grossing app of the month.

# Three Key Trends Behind the Rankings

## 1. Games Still Lead, But Revenue Sources Are Diversifying

Games remain the largest revenue category thanks to in-app purchases and live-service operations.

However, content platforms, streaming services, subscription tools, and online recruitment apps are steadily increasing their contribution to the overall market.

This suggests that China's mobile economy is becoming less dependent on gaming alone.

---

## 2. Non-Gaming Apps Continue to Grow

Eight of the top fifteen apps are non-gaming products, covering multiple categories.

| Category | Representative Apps | Primary Monetization |
|-----------|--------------------|----------------------|
| Short Video | Douyin Select | Advertising, Live Streaming, E-commerce |
| Video Streaming | Tencent Video, iQIYI | Subscription |
| Music | QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music | Membership & Live Streaming |
| Productivity | CapCut | Subscription |
| Recruitment | BOSS Zhipin | Enterprise Services |

These products all share two important characteristics:

- High user engagement
- Clear monetization models

Rather than relying on one-time purchases, they generate recurring revenue through subscriptions, memberships, advertising, or platform transactions.

---

## 3. AI Features Are Becoming Part of Sustainable Businesses

CapCut stands out as one of the strongest examples.

Its success isn't driven by AI itself, but by how AI improves creators' workflows.

Users are willing to subscribe because the product saves time, reduces editing complexity, and increases productivity.

This reflects a broader trend in AI applications:

People rarely pay for AI alone—they pay for better outcomes.

# What This Means for App Developers

The June rankings highlight three important lessons.

### Focus on Solving Real Problems

The highest-grossing apps all address frequent user needs, whether entertainment, productivity, or content consumption.

### Strong Monetization Beats Large Downloads

Download volume doesn't always translate into revenue.

Subscription products, creator tools, and enterprise services often monetize more efficiently than consumer apps with massive user bases.

### Use Market Data Before Building

Before developing a new product, developers should evaluate:

- Revenue rankings
- Download trends
- Historical growth
- Publisher concentration
- Competitive landscape

Market data can significantly reduce product risk and reveal underserved opportunities.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where does the revenue data come from?

The rankings are based on revenue estimates generated by **Appark** using publicly available data from the Apple App Store and Google Play. Revenue from third-party Android app stores in China is not included.

## Why is revenue ranking different from download ranking?

Downloads measure user acquisition, while revenue reflects monetization performance.

An app with fewer users can generate higher revenue through subscriptions, in-app purchases, or enterprise services.

## Which app categories monetize best?

Based on the June 2026 rankings, the strongest categories include:

- Mobile games
- Short-form video
- Video streaming
- Music services
- Productivity tools
- Enterprise software

# Conclusion

The June 2026 rankings show that China's mobile app market is becoming increasingly diversified.
